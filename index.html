That is a brand most clinics would pay for. The opportunity

Women's wellness is your wide-open lane.

Injectables on the Sunshine Coast is a crowded, expensive fight. Pelvic floor, bladder confidence, core strength after children and through menopause is not. Only one other clinic on the Coast has a TESLAChair, and their whole story is body transformation. Yours is a nurse who has moved into holistic women's health and talks about perimenopausal skin like a friend would.

The concern, not the chair

Cold audiences don't search "TESLAChair". They search "why do I leak when I sneeze", "pelvic floor after baby", "menopause bladder". Every campaign, page and post leads with the concern the woman recognises. The chair is the answer in the consult.

Your walking-distance audience

World Gym opens two doors from you in October. The high-rises on the water are across the road. A three to five kilometre campaign radius around Ocean Street, women 35 to 65, is a small, warm, affordable audience nobody else is speaking to about this.

Injectables, PRP, bioremodelling and skin stay on the menu. They become the second visit. Women's wellness is the door.

Quick wins - things you can do this week

Free, fast, and already discussed on the call.

None of this needs us. These fix the leaks before a single ad runs.

Pre-frame the $50 before the booking link

People hit Timely, see the fee and time out. Add one warm line on the website booking page and a pinned post: "I take a $50 booking fee for new consults. It comes straight off your treatment, and it's refundable with 48 hours notice." Expectation set, drop-off gone.

Keep your face on the profile

Ignore the advice to swap your photo for a logo. People follow people. Your patients check in on you when you go quiet, which is the exact relationship a logo can't have. Your socials are genuinely good. Keep going.

Google Business Profile: get unstuck

Alicia is auditing why verification keeps failing. Before the workshop, make sure the clinic name, the Ocean Street address and the phone number are identical on the website footer, Facebook, Instagram and the Timely booking page. Mismatches are the most common reason the video verification bounces.

Walk into World Gym with a one-pager

You've already emailed the manager and the physio next door. Give them something to hand out: a single page, concern-led, "pelvic floor and core support for members, first session with Agnes" with a QR code. Partnerships move when there's a piece of paper.

The plan

Three marketing moves. Three systems. That's the whole thing.

You said if you don't do something the moment you decide, it won't happen. So this is deliberately short.

Marketing

1. The women's wellness campaign on Meta

A lead-form campaign to women 35 to 65 within a few kilometres of Ocean Street. Concern-led hooks in your voice, filmed on your existing content day. Leads land in a CRM and get followed up automatically. This replaces the one-week boosts.

2. Google, once it lets you in

Verification fixed first. Then Search ads for the people already typing "cosmetic nurse Maroochydore", "pelvic floor treatment Sunshine Coast", "PRP Sunshine Coast". Set up properly once, tracked into Timely, then left running.

3. Education content that works harder

Your weekly filming day stays. We add Claude, trained on your voice and AHPRA rules, so every video becomes a caption, an ad, a newsletter section and a Maryborough reminder without you rewriting it four times.

Systems

1. An assistant on your DMs, email and missed calls

Trained on your voice and your FAQs. It answers the 7am enquiry, explains the $50 the way you would, and sends the Timely link. You still get the personal ones. You stop getting all of them.

2. A CRM that catches the timeouts

Every enquiry and every abandoned booking gets a record. The person who timed out at the deposit gets a warm text from your number within minutes, not a message from you that night. You see who booked and who didn't on one screen.

3. Recall, Maryborough and the monthly email

Your one education email a month keeps going, but from a proper system. Maryborough dates go out from your own mobile number, which fixes most of the opt-out problem. Treatment plan reminders and review requests run on their own.
