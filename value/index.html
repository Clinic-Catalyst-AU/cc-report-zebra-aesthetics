Two things that have to change

Google has to verify you, and the deposit cannot be the first thing they meet

Your Google Business Profile has bounced verification for months, and until it clears you cannot run Google Search at all. It is the first job in the 30 days: clinic name, Ocean Street address and phone made identical across your website footer, Facebook, Instagram and Timely, then resubmitted. That is what Google is checking for.

The $50 consult fee is costing you bookings, in your words: as soon as people see they have to pay, they stop. The fee stays. What changes is when they meet it. The assessment comes first, then the assistant explains what the $50 is and that it comes off the treatment, and only then does the booking link arrive.

Enquiries and follow-up run through the CRM we build, which is where the pixel and the tracking live, so every booking is attributed back to the ad or the search that created it. Timely stays your calendar.
